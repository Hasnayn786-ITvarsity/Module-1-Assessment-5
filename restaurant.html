DINE AT THE ZOO

Dine At The Zoo is our outdoor restaurant that offers a more relaxed and gourmet dining experience. It is beautifully set under thatch with views of the lake and the zoo gardens. The menu includes burgers, steaks, salads, pastas and light meals for everyone.

It is the perfect place for a romantic lunch or a family celebration. The atmosphere is calm and peaceful, away from the fast-food rush, and our chefs use fresh, local ingredients. You can sit and watch the ducks on the lake while enjoying your meal.

The restaurant also has a kids' menu and a great selection of desserts and coffees to finish off your meal.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT

Dine at The Zoo is located behind the insect house, overlooking the lost forest lake.
